Farmacia Boots — Abbiadori

Pick up 100% mineral SPF 50+. Ask for “protezione solare 100% minerale SPF 50+”. Avène Mineral Fluid / Mineral Cream are good products to ask whether they have in stock.

09:45–10:30 · Village + coffee

San Pantaleo + Café La Bruttea

Walk the little granite village and have an espresso or cappuccino without turning it into another meal. Café La Bruttea is the safer Tuesday choice: inexpensive, well rated and open from early morning.

Piazza Arborea 5 · Rated 4.5/5 from 100+ reviews at time of planning.

11:00–12:45 · Main beach

★ Spiaggia del Principe

Prioritize this one. It’s the classic Costa Smeralda combination of pale sand, granite and very clear turquoise water. Park above the beach and expect a short walk down.

Give yourselves 90–105 minutes: swim, relax, and walk across the central rocks to see both sides.

13:15–14:30 · Lunch

🍽 Ai Due Mari — Pevero Golf

My pick for lunch. This is a much better fit for your day than eating early in San Pantaleo: scenic, relaxed and close to the afternoon beaches. The menu mixes Sardinian flavours with seafood, pasta, salads, grilled dishes and lighter options.

Recent feedback especially mentions the views, grilled octopus, pasta specials and good service. The restaurant looks across the golf course between Pevero and Cala di Volpe.

Via del Golf 20, Cala di Volpe · Current listings show Tuesday service through the afternoon; lunch last seating has been listed as 16:00. Typical spend roughly €30–80 pp depending on ordering.

14:50–16:00 · Second swim

Capriccioli

A great contrast with Principe: several smaller coves, shallow clear water and rounded granite. It’s particularly good for an easy post-lunch swim.

If you’re perfectly happy at Principe, skip this and linger there instead.

Directions →
16:30–17:30 · Optional short hike

Pevero Health Trail

Do only a 45–60 minute out-and-back from the Grande Pevero side. You’ll get Mediterranean scrub, granite and coastal views without committing to the full trail network or turning the day into a workout.

Wear trainers rather than flip-flops. If it still feels hot, skip the hike and go straight to the beach.

17:45 onward · Easy finish

Grande Pevero

Finish with a walk or final swim at the broad Pevero bay. It’s a relaxed place to stay as the light softens before heading back to Hotel Balocco.

Beach priority

1 · Spiaggia del Principe — the must-see today.
2 · Capriccioli — best add-on for another swim.
3 · Grande Pevero — best paired with the coastal walk and late-day light.
4 · Liscia Ruja — excellent, but save it for another day rather than overloading this loop.

If you want to simplify

Hotel Balocco → Boots Abbiadori → San Pantaleo → Spiaggia del Principe → Ai Due Mari → Grande Pevero → Hotel.

Drop Capriccioli and the hike. This is the relaxed version and still gives you a superb first day.
